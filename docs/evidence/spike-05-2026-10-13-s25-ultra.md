## Result

The stack works on the S25 Ultra exactly as on the emulator, and the key is held by the trusted environment. Both KDFs finish well inside a few seconds. Argon2id was faster than PBKDF2 here, which is the opposite of what would be expected from a hardened parameter set, so the Argon2id memory cost (19 MiB) is at the OWASP minimum and could be raised before release.

**Limits**

1. Z Flip 5 not run.
2. "Restart" is still a close and reopen with a new key provider, not a process kill. A force-stop and reboot check against stored data is still needed.
3. Timings are for three rows; large databases are not measured.
4. The KDF choice is not made. Both work; this is the user's decision.

## Decision or change this causes

- Nothing new. Supports using Room plus SQLCipher with a Keystore-wrapped key for TASK-101.
- Open for the user: PBKDF2 or Argon2id for the portable export, once the Z Flip 5 timing is known.
